Clipfinder Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Summary

Clipfinder is a personal desktop tool. It runs only on the owner's computer, has no server, and does not collect, sell or share data about anyone.

2. Data Clipfinder handles

  • TikTok: when the owner connects their TikTok account, Clipfinder receives an access token and basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) through the user.info.basic scope. It uses video.upload and video.publish only to upload the owner's own edited videos to the owner's own account.
  • YouTube: an OAuth token for the owner's own channel, used only to upload videos.
  • Videos and metadata: the edited video files, titles, descriptions and hashtags, kept on the owner's computer.

3. Where data is stored

All tokens and files are stored locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except to TikTok and YouTube through their official APIs, when the owner uploads a video.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

5. Deleting data

The owner can disconnect at any time by deleting the local token files or revoking access in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services) or in their Google account settings.

6. Children

Clipfinder is not directed at children and is not used by anyone other than its adult owner.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
